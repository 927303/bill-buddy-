Privacy

Your privacy matters to us. Bill Buddy only asks for the information needed to manage your reminders. Your personal reminder and financial records remain private and are never publicly visible. We do not ask for your bank passwords, UPI PIN or OTP.

What we store

Your name, Gmail, language and theme, and the bills, udhar entries and payment requests you add. Each user can only see their own data.

Login

You log in with a one-time code sent to your Gmail. One Gmail is one account.

Payments and refunds

Plans are paid by UPI and verified manually by the owner. A transaction ID or screenshot alone does not activate a plan. For payment problems or refund requests, write to the support email below.

Delete my data

Email us from your registered Gmail and we will delete your account and data.

Terms

Bill Buddy is a reminder tool, not a bank or payment app. Please keep your reminders accurate. We may suspend accounts that misuse the service.
